# 24. Communication Plan

> **Part C: Execution** · Section 24 of 33 · Lead: Change Lead + Internal Communications · Related: §20.4 wave lifecycle, §23

## 24.1 Communication principles

1. **Right message, right audience, right channel, right time.** Clinicians don't read long emails, so use huddles, posters, and badge-reader stickers.
2. **Specific dates and actions.** Every user communication answers: *what*, *when (for me)*, *what do I need to do*, *where to get help*.
3. **One voice.** All program comms go through the Change Lead. No ad-hoc IT emails to all staff.
4. **Plain language.** No "Entra", "Intune", "co-management" in user comms. Say "your new sign-in", "your files in OneDrive".
5. **Two-way.** Every comm includes a feedback route. FAQs are updated weekly from tickets and questions.

## 24.5 Key messages by persona

| Persona | Key message |
|---|---|
| Executives | "We're cutting our biggest cyber risk and the time it takes to get a working device from days to hours, without disrupting care." |
| Clinicians | "Faster, more reliable sign-in on shared workstations; replacement devices that are ready in minutes; nothing changes in how you use the EHR." |
| Knowledge workers | "Your files follow you on any device, sign in with your face or PIN, and print anywhere, with no VPN." |
| Remote staff | "No more VPN for daily work; a replacement laptop ships to your door ready to go." |
| Research | "You keep the tools you need. Elevation on demand, secure by default, Linux and macOS supported." |
| IT | "We're moving from maintaining servers to engineering experiences. Here's your training and certification path." |

## 24.6 Crisis and incident communications
