# Risk Analysis, Part 1: Commonly Forgotten Items and Hidden Dependencies

> **Risk Analysis** · IDs **R-101 to R-149** · Scoring per §26.1 (Probability H/M/L; Severity Critical/High/Medium/Low) · Feeds the RAID log (App-G)
>
> These are the risks that **don't** show up in a vendor's migration checklist. They come out of real enterprise migrations, where they caused the delays. Each one has a **detection method**, because a risk you can't detect is one you can't manage.

---

## RA-1. What organizations commonly forget

| ID | Forgotten item | Why it gets missed | Impact | Prob. | Severity | Mitigation | Contingency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-101 | **Apple Push Notification (APNs) certificate, ADE token, VPP token expiry**. Annual renewal with the *same* Apple ID. | Owned by one person; renewal reminder goes to a departed admin's mailbox | Renewing the APNs certificate with a different Apple ID forces re-enrollment of every iOS/macOS device (~3,000) | M | **High** | Shared mailbox Apple ID; expiry alerts at 60/30/7 days (§32.4); runbook | If wrong Apple ID is used: Apple support case; worst case mass re-enrollment |
| R-102 | **Autopilot deregistration on disposal/resale** | Asset disposal is a different team | Disposed devices re-enroll into tenant (security), or resold devices are locked to Contoso (vendor disputes) | H | Medium | Disposal SOP step + Graph automation tied to the asset-retire state | Bulk deregistration; vendor coordination |
| R-103 | **Time zones and regional settings on Autopilot-built devices** | Old images were set per site | Wrong time on clinical documentation timestamps (EHR uses server time, but local apps may not) | M | Medium | Settings catalog time zone by site group; auto time zone with location services if permitted | Remediation script |
| R-104 | **Device naming conventions used by other systems** (ISE rules, firewall rules, printer ACLs, licensing, SIEM parsing, CMDB matching) | Naming is treated as cosmetic | Autopilot names (`CH-%SERIAL%`) break hostname-based rules | H | High | Inventory hostname consumers (§11.6 field); update rules to group/cert/device-ID based | Rename template change; temporary allow rules |
| R-105 | **Local printers & USB peripherals using drivers from the old image** | Drivers were baked into the WIM | Signature pads, label printers, and dictation microphones don't work post-Autopilot | H | High | Driver Win32 packages as app dependencies; clinical sim lab validation | Spare pre-provisioned devices; driver hotfix package |
| R-106 | **Browser favorites, saved passwords, and IE-era bookmarks** | Lived in roaming profiles / local | User frustration on day 1; password resets | H | Low | Edge sign-in sync (enterprise sync enabled); communicate; export/import guidance | Desk-side help |
| R-107 | **Outlook PST files on H: and local drives** | "Archive" habits | Data loss on wipe; PSTs on OneDrive cause sync issues | M | Medium | PST discovery scan; import to Exchange Online archive (Purview PST import) before Path B; block PST to OneDrive (sync exclusion) | Recover from H: read-only snapshot |
| R-108 | **Sticky Notes, Desktop shortcuts to UNC paths, mapped-drive-dependent Office links** | Invisible to scans | Broken links; tickets | H | Low | KFM includes Desktop; link-fixing guidance; Office "Edit links" remediation for key templates | Floor-walkers |
| R-109 | **Shared mailboxes and calendar delegates on shared devices** | Shared-device redesign focuses on login | Clinical scheduling disrupted | M | Medium | Test shared mailbox access in clinical pilot (Outlook on web on shared devices) | Workarounds documented |
| R-110 | **Service accounts used for Autologon on kiosks/signage** | "Set and forget" years ago | Kiosks fail after password changes or AD cleanup | H | Medium | Inventory kiosks; move to Assigned Access with local kiosk account via Intune | Re-image with self-deploying Autopilot |
| R-111 | **Scheduled tasks on PCs running as domain accounts** (e.g., nightly data pulls on lab PCs) | Not in any app inventory | Silent failure after Entra join; data gaps | M | High | Endpoint scan (§15.2 #10) | Path D for affected devices |
| R-112 | **Wake-on-LAN / after-hours maintenance windows** used by MECM | Assumed; Intune has no WoL | Updates not installed on powered-off desktops; compliance drops | M | Medium | Autopatch deadlines + BIOS auto-power-on schedule (OEM tools); Delivery Optimization | Accept compliance grace for clinical shut-down devices |
| R-113 | **BitLocker PIN / pre-boot auth expectations** | Some policies required TPM+PIN | TPM+PIN breaks silent Autopilot encryption and remote reboots | M | Medium | TPM-only for standard; TPM+PIN only for high-risk travel devices with documented process | Recovery key retrieval process |
| R-114 | **Certificate expiry of the CMG server certificate, AD FS token-signing certificate, Cert Connector, Universal Print connector, Private Network Connector certs** during the bridge | Bridge components have short attention spans | Outage of a transitional component | M | High | Certificate expiry register; monitoring | Emergency renewal runbooks |
| R-115 | **Licence assignment for shared-device users** (F3 vs. desktop apps; MDE coverage) | Licensing considered per user, devices forgotten | Missing protection/apps on shared PCs | M | High | LIC-2/LIC-3 validation (§29.3) | Temporary E3 step-up for affected users |
| R-116 | **Windows Hello for Business on shared devices** | WHfB is per user per device | Hundreds of WHfB containers on shared PCs; TPM key slot exhaustion; slow first sign-in | M | Medium | **Disable WHfB on shared clinical devices** (badge/CBA/FIDO2 instead) via device-group policy | Clear containers with remediation |
| R-117 | **Language packs, accessibility tools, and multilingual users** | Image previously included them | Accessibility compliance issues | L | Medium | Language/feature-on-demand through Intune (settings catalog / Win32); accessibility app set | Manual install by Tier 2 |
| R-118 | **Old OneDrive/SharePoint sync of non-Contoso tenants** (affiliates) | Cross-tenant usage | Data leakage; blocked by tenant restrictions | M | Medium | Tenant allow-list exceptions for affiliate tenants with approval; cross-tenant access settings | Access through browser |
| R-119 | **The "IT closet" estate**: test machines, training-room PCs, conference room PCs, nurse-station label-printer hosts, digital signage players | Not assigned to any department → no wave | Left on Win10/AD/MECM after MECM decommission | H | Medium | Wave "orphans" sweep (MDE device discovery + MECM inventory); dedicated W-orphan wave M13 | Isolation VLAN; replacement |
| R-120 | **Time-limited trials going into production** (Cloud PKI trial CA = software keys; MDI trial) | Pilots start on trial licences | Re-issuing all certs later; lost configs | M | Medium | Production CAs only after licensing; tracker of trials with end dates | Re-issue plan |

## RA-2. Hidden dependencies

### RA-2.1 Dependency risk table

| ID | Hidden dependency | Example in a healthcare estate | Impact | Prob. | Severity | Detection | Mitigation | Contingency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-121 | **Applications hard-coded to DC names/IPs** | LIS interface engine binds to `dc03`; pharmacy dispensing cabinet console queries `10.1.1.10` | Breaks on DC demotion/IP change (Q2) | H | **Critical** | DNS logs, firewall flows to DC IPs, 2889 events, config greps | DNS abstraction (`ldap.corp…` VIP) (§15.6 P-B); re-point before any DC change | Keep the DC (or its IP on a new DC) alive; re-IP with alias |
| R-122 | **LDAP simple (cleartext) binds** | MFP address books, door access system, older HR/time & attendance, VPN appliances, web apps' "AD login" | Credential exposure; break when LDAP signing/channel binding is enforced | H | **Critical** | Event 2889 (client IP + account), MDI cleartext-credential report | LDAPS with service account + DNS alias; SAML/SCIM where possible | Delay signing enforcement for specific DCs (exception, with compensating monitoring) |
| R-123 | **Kerberos-dependent services** (IWA web apps, SQL Windows auth, file shares, Citrix StoreFront with domain pass-through) | EHR reporting portal, PACS web viewer, intranet, SSRS | Fail from Entra-joined devices if no line-of-sight or SPN issues; NTLM fallback hides misconfiguration until NTLM is restricted | H | High | 4769 by SPN; `klist` on test devices; MDI | Cloud Kerberos trust; register SPNs for FQDN/aliases; Private Access for remote; App Proxy KCD | Path D for affected users; publish app via AVD |
| R-124 | **NTLM-only applications** (IP-address URLs, non-domain servers, legacy appliances) | Medical device management consoles accessed by IP, vendor-hosted on-prem web apps | Fail when NTLM restricted; credential relay risk | H | High | 4776/8004 audit; MDI legacy protocol report | Use FQDN + SPN to enable Kerberos; vendor upgrade | NTLM exception lists (server-specific) |
| R-125 | **Computer-account-based authorization** | SQL logins for `CONTOSO\WS-*$`; shares ACL'd to `Domain Computers`; proxy auth by machine; NPS policies by computer group | **Fails on Entra join** (no computer object) | M | **Critical** | ACL scans for computer SIDs; SQL login audit; NPS/ISE policy export | Redesign to user/app identity (§15.6 P-E) | Path D / Hybrid for those devices |
| R-126 | **SMB shares used by apps (not people)** | Lab instrument drops results to `\\labfs\inbox$`; EHR batch print to file share; scanning stations | Breaks on share migration to SharePoint | H | High | Migration Manager scan + file server SMB session audit (Event 5140/5145 sampling) + owner interviews | Keep on Azure Files/on-prem (§12.3 decision tree) | Restore share (read-write) from read-only state |
| R-127 | **Scanners/MFPs**: scan-to-SMB with a domain service account, LDAP address book, SMTP relay authenticating to on-prem Exchange/relay | 1,600 MFPs | Scanning stops on share retirement/LDAP enforcement/relay changes | H | High | MFP fleet management export, 2889, relay logs | Universal Print scan to OneDrive/SPO or vendor cloud connector; SMTP relay to Exchange Online (connector with cert/IP) | Keep a small scan landing share on Azure Files temporarily |
| R-128 | **Scheduled tasks/services under service accounts on servers** consuming user-shares or DCs | Nightly ETL from department share into data warehouse | Breaks on share migration or service-account cleanup | H | High | Server scan for scheduled tasks `RunAs` domain accounts; MDI logon events per account | Re-point to SharePoint via Graph (app registration with cert) or Azure Files; gMSA | Temporarily re-enable account / restore share |
| R-129 | **Group Policy-delivered dependencies on apps** (registry keys an app expects, trusted sites zones for IWA, ODBC DSNs, environment variables) | EHR-adjacent apps reading `HKLM\Software\Policies\Vendor` | App misbehaves post-Entra join because GPO no longer applies | H | High | GPO reports + app owner L2 testing on Entra-joined devices | Recreate in Intune (ADMX ingestion / remediation); Edge site-to-zone assignment (`IntranetZone` for Kerberos auto-logon) | Path D |
| R-130 | **Trusted Sites / Intranet zone configuration for Kerberos auto-logon** | IWA sites prompting for credentials | Users see auth prompts; perceived "SSO broken" | H | Medium | Test E2E-07 | Edge policies: `AuthServerAllowlist`, Site to Zone Assignment List | User guidance |
| R-131 | **DFS Namespace root and links** embedded in apps/docs (`\\corp.contoso-health.org\dfs\…`) | Namespace survives server moves, so everyone used it | Domain-based DFS requires AD; breaks in Phase 6 | M | Medium | DFS-N export; document link scans | Repoint to SharePoint/Azure Files; keep DFS-N until Phase 6 | Keep namespace on residual DCs |
| R-132 | **AD group nesting used for app authorization** (on-prem apps reading `memberOf`) | HR app roles via AD groups | Breaks if groups move to cloud-only SOA | M | Medium | App dependency profile; LDAP query logs (MDI) | Keep synced (or Cloud Sync group writeback) for on-prem-consumed groups | Revert group SOA |
| R-133 | **Password-based legacy integrations** (apps storing user AD passwords, SSO tools capturing passwords) | Badge-SSO vendor password vaulting for EHR; legacy web forms | Passwordless (WHfB) users don't know/never type passwords → vault breaks | M | **High** | Vendor discussion; pilot | Vendor's passwordless/Entra integration; keep password for EHR via vault; CBA | Exclude users from "passwordless-only" until fixed |
| R-134 | **RADIUS/NPS for network devices and VPN using AD** | Switch admin login, Wi-Fi admin, VPN | Admin access to network breaks on AD reduction | M | Medium | NPS/ISE policies | Entra-backed RADIUS (via ISE with SAML/Entra integration) or keep in residual AD | Local break-glass on network devices |
| R-135 | **Exchange hybrid / AD attribute mastering** (proxyAddresses, mailbox provisioning) | On-prem Exchange management server for recipient management | Can't decommission AD/Exchange management cleanly | M | Medium | Exchange configuration review | Exchange management tools only (no server) where supported; plan SOA transfer when Microsoft supports it | Keep one management host |
| R-136 | **Time sync hierarchy (PDC emulator)** for medical/lab devices | Lab analyzers sync time from DCs | Time drift → documentation integrity issues (Part 11) | M | High | NTP logs / device configs | Dedicated NTP appliances / network core | Keep NTP role on residual DC |
| R-137 | **DHCP options/reservations for medical devices** on DC-hosted DHCP | Infusion pump reservations | Device connectivity loss on DHCP migration | M | High | DHCP export; biomed engineering review | Migrate reservations with validation; biomed sign-off | Roll back DHCP scope |
| R-138 | **Hard-coded UNC paths in Office templates, macros, Access DBs, Excel links** | Finance models linking to `\\fs02\finance$\…` | Broken models at month-end | H | Medium | Link scan tools; owner attestation | Re-link before cutover; schedule finance cutover outside close | Temporary read-write source share |
| R-139 | **Licensing servers (FlexLM, etc.) checking domain membership or hostnames** | Research/engineering software | Licence denial after Entra join/rename | M | Medium | App profile | Vendor reconfiguration (user-based or Entra-aware licensing) | Path D |
| R-140 | **Dependency on `%LOGONSERVER%`, NETLOGON scripts, or `net use` in app launchers** | Legacy batch launchers | Fails on Entra-joined devices | M | Medium | Script scan | Rewrite launchers | Path D |

### RA-2.2 Hidden dependency discovery checklist

- [ ] DC Security, Directory Service (2887/2889), and NTLM operational logs collected ≥ 60 days.
- [ ] MDI deployed, with LDAP, legacy protocol, and cleartext reports reviewed.
- [ ] DNS query logging identifies clients resolving specific DC hostnames.
- [ ] NetFlow to DC IPs on 389/636/3268/3269/88/445/135 analyzed by source subnet.
- [ ] SPN inventory mapped to owners.
- [ ] Endpoint scan for scheduled tasks/services under domain accounts, UNC paths, `%LOGONSERVER%`.
- [ ] Server scan for scheduled tasks and services under domain accounts.
- [ ] Share ACLs and SQL logins scanned for computer accounts.
- [ ] MFP/scanner fleet configuration exported (scan destinations, LDAP, SMTP).
- [ ] Medical device / biomed inventory reviewed for AD, DNS, DHCP, NTP, and SMB use.
- [ ] Office documents scanned for external links to UNC paths (department shares).
- [ ] App owners have attested the dependency profile for 100 % of in-scope apps.
