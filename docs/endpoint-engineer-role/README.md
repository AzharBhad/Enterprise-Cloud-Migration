## The five things that matter most in your role

1. **Prove certificate-based Wi-Fi for Entra-joined devices by M4.** It's the critical path (§16).
2. **Treat KFM verification as a hard gate before any wipe.** Data loss destroys user trust faster than anything else.
3. **Migrate intent, not GPOs.** Baseline-first, with each GPO unlinked the same day its setting area moves to Intune.
4. **Keep Autopilot thin and measurable.** ≤ 6 blocking apps, with success rate and time tracked daily.
5. **Put everything in Git and have every change reviewed.** That's your rollback, your audit evidence, and your portfolio.
